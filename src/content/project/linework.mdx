## Overview

Linework simplifies the path from inquiry to confirmed appointment, reducing back-and-forth communication with practical automation tailored to solo artists.

It integrates with tools artists already use (email, Google Calendar, Venmo/Zelle) while
avoiding unnecessary complexity, with a privacy-conscious architecture aimed at reducing
friction in the booking process.

Most tattoo booking workflows happen across fragmented channels: Instagram DMs, email threads,
notes apps, spreadsheets, and payment links.

Linework centralizes this process into a single structured workflow designed specifically for
solo tattoo artists.

The primary design goal is reducing the operational overhead of managing bookings while preserving
flexibility for artists who each have slightly different processes.

## Core Concepts

* structured intake forms for collecting project details
* conversation history tied directly to client projects
* templated responses for common communication
* scheduling coordination via Google Calendar
* deposit tracking with flexible payment methods
* internal notes for project context
* lightweight CRM functionality without enterprise complexity

The system is intentionally designed to support iterative rollout of features, allowing artists
to adopt the platform without needing to fully change their workflow all at once.

## Tech

TypeScript, Next.js, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Drizzle ORM, PlanetScale/Postgres,
Cloudflare R2 for file storage.
