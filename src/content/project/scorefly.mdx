## Overview

Scorefly is a darts-first scoring app for casual games. It is built to keep scoring fast, reduce mental math, and make the state of the match easy to read from a phone or a table-side iPad.

The core product promise is simple: pick a game, add players, and score every throw without slowing down the match. Clear scoreboards, large tap targets, checkout help, and game-specific layouts keep totals, marks, turns, busts, and progress visible without making players do the bookkeeping themselves.

## Why I Built It

Casual darts gets slower when the table has to track every total, mark, turn, and rule variation by hand. X01 requires subtraction and checkout awareness. Cricket needs marks, ownership, and points. Other casual games add their own shape to the score.

Scorefly handles that scoring work so players can keep their attention on the board and the group. The app is intentionally focused on game-night scoring instead of training, league management, smart-board integrations, or deep stats.

## Core Concepts

- X01 scoring for 301, 501, and variants with checkout help and bust handling
- Cricket variants including standard, cutthroat, no-points, and flexible targets
- Bermuda and Tic Tac Toe as additional casual game modes
- Phone layouts for quick scoring from one hand
- Table-side iPad layouts with large controls and readable scoreboards
- Scorefly Plus for more games, flexible rule options, expanded setup, and more players
